Rock for Hope isn’t just a gig, it’s our way of raising vital funds for the Gift of Hope Trust, our charity that works to ensure every child has access to education, no matter where they’re growing up.

Gift of Hope partners with grassroots organisations in India, Ghana, Malawi and Uganda to build and support sustainable education projects, from classrooms and toilet blocks to safe teacher accommodation and vocational training colleges.

Every band that takes to the stage gives their time for free, ensuring the majority of ticket sales goes directly towards creating long-term impact on the ground.

This is why we Rock for Hope. And we’d love you to be part of it.

Landline:

The Landline were formed in North London in 2021 of a collective desire to play the finest tunes from one of rock and pop music’s greatest decades: the Nineties. Naturally, this encompasses many of the big Britpop bands of the time – from Oasis, Blur and Pulp to The Bluetones, Travis and Supergrass – but also the best of the Madchester scene (including the Stone Roses and Happy Mondays), some great dance cuts (Fatboy Slim, Chemical Brothers), several much-loved alternative acts (Eels, Belle & Sebastian, Primal Scream) and even the occasional foray into classic indie from other eras (Joy Division, Arctic Monkeys).

Firefly:

Firefly burst out of the Camden youth scene in late 2025 and have been lighting up the North London circuit ever since. As prominent members of the INTRA collective they have headlined sold out shows at Camden’s iconic Jazz Cafe, The Camden Club and more with their mix of Motown soul, vintage grooves and modern pop funk anthems.

The band are Miya (vocals), Max (guitar), Humphrey (keys), April (bass) and Alfred (drums).

Gift of Noise:

Gift of Noise is an energetic and versatile eight-piece band hailing from London. Known for their powerful live performances and eclectic mix of genres, they bring a unique blend of talent to every stage. With dynamic vocals, rich instrumentation, and a diverse repertoire spanning rock, pop, soul, and more, Gift of Noise captivates audiences at events across the city and beyond. Their ability to seamlessly fuse different styles ensures an unforgettable musical experience, making them one of London’s most exciting live acts.

Their setlist spans a diverse mix of genres, blending iconic hits from Motown, rock, indie, grime, and hip-hop. The inclusion of The Jackson 5, Muse, The Killers, Stormzy, Eminem, a-ha, Journey. Together, this genre mix creates a dynamic and versatile experience, appealing to a wide audience.
