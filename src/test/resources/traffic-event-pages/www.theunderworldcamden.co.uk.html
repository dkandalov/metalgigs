Alive: A Tribute to Pearl Jam – “The World’s Greatest Pearl Jam Tribute”.

Alive: A Tribute to Pearl Jam give the most accurate and engaging performance of Pearl Jam’s music you will find anywhere, delivered with the typical Pearl Jam intensity they are renowned for.

Choosing songs from the entire Pearl Jam catalogue and some of the covers Pearl Jam themselves play, all culminate in a tribute band that becomes more than just a tribute.

Alive: A Tribute to Pearl Jam is the only “International Touring Tribute to Pearl Jam”, firmly establishing themselves as the definitive tribute to the band – completing numerous sold out UK and European tours and headlining major festivals along the way, performing to thousands of dedicated fans from around the world.
