Tickets are now available for DREAMDNVR at The Grace!

Swedish-born producer, songwriter, and artist Philip Tillström is the creative force behind DREAMDNVR one of modern pop’s fastest-rising new names. First gaining international recognition as one half of the duo Hearts & Colors. As a songwriter and producer, DREAMDNVR has achieved over 500 million global streams, earning a reputation for emotionally charged melodies and genre-blending pop with worldwide appeal. Now stepping fully into the spotlight as an artist, he is quickly establishing himself as one of the most exciting breakout acts in the space. Momentum around the project has surged, generating 50+ million social media views in the past month alone and attracting hundreds of thousands of new followers across platforms. Through a powerful blend of music, visuals, and storytelling, DREAMDNVR has built a rapidly growing international fanbase. Fresh off a successful European support run, DREAMDNVR now enters a major new chapter with his first-ever headline European tour this October a defining milestone that marks his evolution from online breakout to fully realized live artist
