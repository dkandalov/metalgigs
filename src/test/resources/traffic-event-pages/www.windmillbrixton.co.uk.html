GROMMET

Emerging from the Swedish underground, Grommet is a four-piece powerhouse bridging the gap between beauty and aggression.

While their heavy yet ethereal style of alternative metal is still uncommon in the Swedish music scene, they’re proving themselves to be a sensation after only four singles.

Since their debut in November 2025, Grommet’s trajectory has been nothing short of unstoppable. The band has cultivated a dedicated global community, over 700 000 monthly listeners on Spotify as of now.

Their single “Half In Red” is sitting at over 7 000 000 streams today. Now the hype around their newest single “Protected Names” (released April 30th) is continuing to push those numbers up rapidly.
