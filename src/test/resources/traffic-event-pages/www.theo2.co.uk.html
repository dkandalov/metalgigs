Event Details

German heavy metal pioneers ACCEPT will celebrate their 50th anniversary live in August 2026, marking a major milestone in the band’s extraordinary five-decade career and offering fans the chance to witness half a century of heavy metal history live on stage.

“It’s great to be back in the UK and to celebrate 50 years of ACCEPT is amazing! Thanks to all who have supported the band over the years and we can’t wait to deliver an extra special show for you all” - Guitarist Wolf Hoffmann:

Special Guests  will be up and coming UK hotshots TAILGUNNER.

Formed in 1976, ACCEPT are recognised as true pioneers of heavy metal, helping to shape the genre for five decades through iconic albums and legendary live performances. Over the past five decades, ACCEPT has sold millions of albums and inspired countless musicians. Their energetic live performances and iconic albums such as "Balls to the Wall", "Restless and Wild" and "Metal Heart" have left a lasting mark on the heavy metal genre.

ACCEPT are
Mark Tornillo – Vocals
Wolf Hoffmann – Guitar
Philip Shouse – Guitar
Martin Motnik – Bass
Christopher Williams – Drums
