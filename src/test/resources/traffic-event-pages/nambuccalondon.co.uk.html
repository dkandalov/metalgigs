My Endless Winter + CRY-99 | NOVEMBER 20TH
My Endless Winter come to London for the first time as part of The Misery Tour!
1000 days after the success of hit single “Therapy”, they released their new song “Misery”, which sets the tone for their 2026 tour in Europe and the UK. Get ready for a night full of heavy riffs, catchy melodies, screams and rap-rock elements, with a setlist that includes bangers from the band’s entire discography, plus covers of favourite bands like Linkin Park and Deftones.
Londoners CRY-99 (emo gaze-pop) open the show!
